# User Journey Maps: AI Maintenance Copilot

This document maps the exact step-by-step experience for 4 key target personas.

---

## Journey 1: Factory Manager (Operational Overview & ROI)
- **Goal**: Understand plant status, financial risk, and downtime prevention in under 30 seconds.
- **Step 1 (Landing)**: Opens application -> Lands on **🏠 Mission Control** in **🏢 Operations Mode**.
- **Step 2 (Situation)**: Sees Top KPIs ("Factory Status: Healthy", "21 Hours Downtime Prevented", "$18,000 Estimated Savings").
- **Step 3 (Insight)**: Reads Today's AI Summary Hero Card ("3 machines require inspection. Machine #17 is highest risk").
- **Step 4 (Action)**: Clicks `Inspect Machine #17` or delegates priority maintenance item.

---

## Journey 2: Maintenance Engineer (Root Cause & Equipment Profile)
- **Goal**: Identify urgent tasks, understand why a machine is failing, and execute maintenance.
- **Step 1**: Clicks **📊 Machine Profiles** or selects Machine #17 from Today's Priority Stack.
- **Step 2**: Inspects current health (18% degraded) and RUL estimate (14 Operating Hours).
- **Step 3**: Views translated root cause explanation (`⬆ High Vibration +42%`, `⬆ Rising Temp +26%`).
- **Step 4**: Clicks `Schedule Maintenance` or `Generate Maintenance Report`.

---

## Journey 3: AI Researcher / IEEE Reviewer (Scientific Validation)
- **Goal**: Inspect mathematical novelty, cross-validation benchmarks, and statistical significance.
- **Step 1**: Switches mode toggle from **🏢 Operations Mode** to **🔬 Research Mode**.
- **Step 2**: Inspects 5-Fold Stratified CV table (99.77% Accuracy, 98.70% Recall, 0.9984 ROC-AUC).
- **Step 3**: Reviews Ablation Matrix ($C_{FP}=10, C_{FN}=500$ showing cost drop from $15,450 to $1,340).
- **Step 4**: Verifies statistical significance ($p < 0.0001$, Cohen's d = 3.421) and inspects 300 DPI vector figures.

---

## Journey 4: Recruiter & Executive (60-Second Evaluation)
- **Goal**: Assess project complexity, frontend aesthetics, and engineering polish.
- **Step 1 (0-15s)**: Impressed by Palantir Foundry dark glassmorphism design system.
- **Step 2 (15-35s)**: Notes zero-jargon decision guidance and persistent AI Maintenance Copilot.
- **Step 3 (35-60s)**: Toggles to Research Mode and Developer Mode to verify backend depth, REST APIs, and Docker orchestration.
