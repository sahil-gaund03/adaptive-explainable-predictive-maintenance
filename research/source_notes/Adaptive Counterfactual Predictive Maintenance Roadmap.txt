1. Research Proposal
Your research will unify cost-sensitive learning, online concept drift detection, and Explainable AI (XAI) in a Smart Manufacturing context. Machine learning systems in operational environments face "concept drift" where data properties change over time, leading to silent failure 1, 2. Simultaneously, black-box AI algorithms in Predictive Maintenance (PdM) limit transparency and operator trust 3. You will build an adaptive system that not only detects operational shifts using an ensemble of statistical detectors but also utilizes Counterfactual Explanations (CFE) to provide actionable repair plans to operators.
2. Literature Review
You must critically synthesize three domains:
* Predictive Maintenance (PdM): PdM research has experienced significant growth since 2014, heavily linked to machine learning 4. Tree-based ensemble methods, particularly XGBoost, excel at capturing non-linear feature interactions and handling mixed data types in tabular PdM data 5, 6.
* Concept Drift: Traditional single-detector approaches often require manual parameter tuning and suffer from unacceptable false alarm rates 7, 8. Advanced streaming architectures employ ensemble mechanisms (e.g., ADWIN, Page-Hinkley, KSWIN, SPC) to monitor prediction residuals and trigger targeted incremental retraining 9-11.
* Explainable AI (XAI): XAI must evolve from merely debugging models to providing context-aware, actionable insights for complex industrial repairs 12, 13.
3. Research Gap
Your paper will address several critical gaps:
* Actionable Insights vs. Debugging: Existing XAI methods focus heavily on parameters and model debugging while neglecting outcome/end-user explainability and actionable insights 14, 15.
* Extreme Misclassification Costs: In real-world PdM, the cost of failing to detect a faulty part (False Negative) can be 50 times higher than conducting a needless check (False Positive) 16. Efficiently addressing massive misclassification cost imbalances in dynamic data streams remains underexplored 17.
* Absence of Standardized XAI Evaluation: The literature lacks universally accepted XAI benchmarks, making it difficult to measure the reliability of explanations 18, 19.
4. Objectives
1. To design a task-agnostic ensemble drift detection architecture with automated calibration that achieves conservative false-positive control 20.
2. To minimize the total maintenance cost of misclassified conditions using a cost-sensitive supervised machine learning approach (gradient boosting trees) 21.
3. To bridge theoretical XAI outputs with actionable maintenance plans by utilizing Counterfactual Explanations (CFE) to answer "What is the minimum required modification to change the prediction of the model?" 22, 23.
5. Research Questions
* RQ1: How can drift detection systems achieve zero false positives while maintaining practical detection latency in cost-sensitive industrial PdM environments? 24
* RQ2: Can the integration of Counterfactual Explanations (CFE) provide actionable, human-centric maintenance recommendations under concept drift? 25, 26
* RQ3: How effectively does incremental model adaptation restore cost-sensitive prediction accuracy while preserving historical knowledge? 27
6. Hypothesis

* H2: Utilizing a cost-sensitive classifier with unequal class weights will significantly lower total maintenance costs compared to a cost-insensitive model 28-30.
* H3: Applying the Explainability Parameters (XPA) framework will quantitatively prove that generated CFEs maintain high model fidelity and stability across concept drift cycles 31, 32.
